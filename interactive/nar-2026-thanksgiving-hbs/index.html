Detached single-family homes remained the most popular property type across all buyer groups, accounting for more than three-quarters (76%) of recent purchases. Married and unmarried couples were especially likely to choose these homes, often seeking space to grow, entertain, and establish long-term roots.

1

While most still opted for detached single-family homes, they were more likely than other buyers to purchase townhouses, mobile or manufactured homes, and other property types.
