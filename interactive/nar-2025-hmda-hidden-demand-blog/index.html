Metropolitan Areas with the Largest Changes in Hidden Demand, 2024–2025

In 2025, hidden demand decreased the most in Little Rock-North Little Rock-Conway, AR (-4.7 pp), Gulfport-Biloxi, MS (-4.4 pp), and Burlington-South Burlington, VT (-4.3 pp). Conversely, the areas where the share of hidden demand increased the most were Elmira, NY (+3.5 pp), Gainesville, FL (+3.0 pp), and Medford, OR (+3.0 pp)
